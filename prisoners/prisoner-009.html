MIKOTO KAYANO

BASIC INFORMATION

A rational individual who is constantly bewildered by the oddity of Milgram. He is trying to get used to these unusual circumstances. Unlike the other members who are aware of their sins, he doesn’t know anything about his own crimes. He firmly believes that it was a slip-up that caused him to be brought here.

PROFILE & ANALYSIS

Kayano Mikoto grew up in a typical, middle-class home. He was sociable, well-liked, picked up most things with ease and excellence, and was generally a well-behaved and easy-going child. He was blessed with many friends and always drew a crowd. He knew how to read a room, how to maintain relationships, and had the ability to make everyone feel at ease. One could say he had a gift, a natural-born talent. He went on to attend and graduate from art school, all without experiencing any major setbacks or disappointments. He then accepted a position to work as a designer for a large advertising agency. His new company was an industry leader, and his peers showered him with impressed and even slightly envious glances. Mikoto had no doubt that his future would be a bright one.

The reality he was met with, however, was not the metropolitan designer-life that he had dreamed of, but an extremely toxic work environment that could even be described as a battlefield. Mikoto had always been able to out-perform expectations in most areas of his life. At his new job, however, he was bombarded with work that far exceeded his capabilities, deadlines upon deadlines, colleagues who somehow seemed to brush off the work as if it were nothing, reprimands, sighs, and disappointed faces...... Often, Mikoto did not even understand the terminologies flying around his workplace, and he survived each day by wearing a faint, uncertain smile on his face. No matter how desperate he felt, he could not utter the words, "please help me". Whenever he received a call from home, he'd lie and say he was doing just fine.

- He could not bring himself to accept it. That he, who had always been first among equals, was now just a burden. The normalization of basically living at work causes Mikoto to reach a breaking point. It was right around the time when he clocked in more than one hundred consecutive days at work. Was he awake or asleep? Was this reality or a dream? Returning to his apartment in a daze, Mikoto collapsed into bed as if his life was slipping away.

Mikoto woke up in his own bed, with surprisingly uplifted spirits. When he checked his phone, it showed that it had been a full 48 hours since he collapsed. That meant he had missed deadlines; "I'm definitely fired", he thought. The mountain of missed calls was proof of that. "I'm going to quit", Mikoto had decided. He was just going to focus on the things that gave him joy, and rest for a bit. He'd have plenty of time to contemplate his life afterwards. All of that stress that had piled up inside of him had melted away. He suddenly realized that he was hungry. Mikoto decided to go get something to eat, and as he was cheerfully getting ready to head out, multiple police officers were already stationed outside his door. They were there to barge in on the serial killer who had randomly taken the lives of 12 people.

Let's rewind the clock to when Mikoto reached his breaking point and fell into a deep slumber. Mikoto gets up. He starts to roam the neighborhood in the dead of the night, metal bat in hand, taking one human life after another. He was letting out a cry that sounded like a furious roar, or maybe a sorrowful wail, or something akin to the first cry of a newborn.
