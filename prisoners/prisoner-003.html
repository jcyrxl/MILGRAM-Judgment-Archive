FUUTA KAJIYAMA

"I'm not my old self anymore"

VIEW TRIAL RECORD ↓

BASIC INFORMATION

Fuuta is a hot-headed guy sho seems to charge in without thinking as he tried to attack ES on his first interrogation. Fuuta has a strong sense of justice and believes that he should be the one who will bring judgment upon evil.

PROFILE & ANALYSIS

Having a very rigid, strong sense of justice, he would use social media to expose the people he found for doing things he perceived as wrong and unjust. Once, he had begun spreading around an inappropriate post by a restaurant employee and posted his identifying information and sent a grievance letter to the restaurant company, which resulted in the employee needing to apologize publicly. After this incident, his habits of social media monitoring were fueled and he became motivated to continue exposing the wrongdoings of people online. His sense of justice grew stronger and he began to make a name for himself in his online circle as a vigilante, which received praise from his peers.

One day, Fuuta found a post by a girl in middle-school, wearing a hat in a shopping mall's clothing store she has not yet bought. Realizing the fame he could draw from by accusing a beautiful-looking girl of breaking the law, he made a post, he cited her post as an offence, accusing her of copyright infringement and immoral conduct among others. His post about her went viral within his circle. After uncovering more information related to this girl, he found out she was his neighbour, which prompted him to dox her by taking a picture of her home and posting it.

Eventually, the girl became aware of this scandal, and tried to respond by posting, saying it was a "matter within the scope personal replication and as such not a crime". However, the controversy only grew and the harassment towards the girl blew out of proportion to the point she could no longer control how she was being treated. Fuuta took pride in the fact he had her cornered.

One day not too long after the incident, he was walking by the girl's house and he noticed that a funeral ceremony for her was taking place. This sight causes him to panic, regretting how far he went to seek justice. He races back home in a nervous sweat and locks himself in his home, racing to his bedroom and curling his body. He throws his phone ringing with constant notifications on his bed.
