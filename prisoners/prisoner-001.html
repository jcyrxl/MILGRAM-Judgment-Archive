HARUKA SAKURAI

"I've only been good at bringing misfortune to others"

BASIC INFORMATION

Haruka Sakurai is a quiet boy who often thinks low of his self. He tends to seek attention from others as he is suffering from a developmental gap, they tend to avoid Haruka. He has find solace in Milgram because he thinks people in Milgram are kinder than the one he knows; they give him nothing but kindness and attention, which his parents didn't gave to him.

PROFILE & ANALYSIS

Compared to other children his age, his overall development was much slower, and he had a harder time adjusting to regular society. Haruka's parents, overprotective of their son, couldn't accept his circumstances, and therefore refused to provide him with the educational accommodations necessary to help him progress. As he grew older, the developmental gap between Haruka and his peers would continue to widen. By the time he was 15 years old, he still could not effectively read kanji. As of this point, his parents started putting less energy into protecting their son, losing hope for his development and abandoning interest in him altogether. They stopped paying attention to him and treated him like he didn't exist. Haruka desperately attempted to grab his parents' attention of him once again through problematic behaviour and troublemaking, but it was never anything serious enough to provoke a reaction out of them. Haruka became fed up of feeling inferior and disconnected from his family and the world, which resulted in him beginning to kill small animals like mice, insects, as well as stray cats and dogs in secret, all while his parents didn't notice. The feeling of murdering small animals affirmed that there was life inferior to him, which encouraged him to escalate these behaviours, testing boundaries to see how far he could go. He began to kill the pets of other people and relished the feeling of their owners wailing in grief, feeling as though he is connected to the world for the first time. As a result of these murders, the security in Haruka's neighbourhood began to tighten, and he was eventually identified by police as the culprit. He was detained and harshly interrogated by them, an experience that excited him due to the attention he was finally receiving. When Haruka's parents were called in for him, seeing their faces intensified his excitement extremely, reaching fever pitch before he finally fainted. The day he was released, the fireworks festival began, where he once again committed murder. However, instead of his victim being another animal, this time he targeted a young girl who had wandered away from her parents. Realizing what he had done, he maladroitly hides her corpse, panicking. For the rest of the night, he couldn't stop thinking about her and what he did, worrying that her future would've been brighter than his, that her life was probably more valuable than his own, and how his life ended up the way it did. He cried about her the nights following her death, ruminating on the future she could've had if it weren't for him. His regret began to tear him apart internally, provoking him to start "strangling the neck of a second child", who was presumably himself.
