MAHIRU SHIINA

"I thought that love won’t lose to anything, but… Being unwanted… is a painful thing to experience, isn’t it?"

VIEW TRIAL RECORD ↓

BASIC INFORMATION

Mahiru is a friendly, caring and a girl who is full of love. She is a hopeless romantic that believes that love is the answer to everything. Mahiru is one of the purest and sweetest person that everyone knows in Milgram (except for Kotoko)

PROFILE & ANALYSIS

Mahiru Shiina was born into an affluent family, and was raised to be the very picture of a sheltered, privileged young woman from wealth. She was to marry a fiancé selected by her family upon graduating university, and was prohibited from having any contact with those of the opposite sex. This severe suppression of romance led to a ballooning idealization of the idea of love.

One day, Mahiru meets a man. He was a university student one year her senior, they met at a social mixer, and the moment their eyes met, Mahiru was convinced that their encounter was destined by the stars. "I was born to love and be loved by this man"; she believed this to her core. He felt the same way, and soon, the distance between them closed. This romance, which she had previously only known through movies and television, transformed everything in her life. A place she had been to a million times before seemed completely transformed when he was with her. This made her realize that her life up till then had no color to it.

The night before Mahiru's planned wedding to her fiancé, the two of them followed through on their plot to run away. A missing person's report was immediately issued. The two of them moved from place to place in order to evade the police closing in on them. The breaking point comes a few months into their desparate escapade. With dwindling resources, they are forced to take refuge in the mountains.

They drank mud water, and ate not just the wild plants but even field mice and insects. Even in this state, Mahiru did not feel hopeless in the least. In fact, she was at the peak of her happiness. She was laying everything out on the line for love, more than in any film or TV show, and she was completely intoxicated in that. The love that Mahiru had always dreamed about was exactly this. There was only her and him in this world. That was all she needed. They huddle together inside a cave, and fall asleep.

When Mahiru awakened, he was not lying next to her. Mahiru panicked, afraid that he had run away without her. She hurried out of the cave, and there, she found him dead, having hung himself from a large tree nearby.

These past few months together, what were his expressions like? She could not remember. Was it just her that believed without a shadow of a doubt that this was love? She didn't know. Was her love so coercive that he couldn't even say the words, "I can't do this anymore"? She didn't know. Because he was such a kind soul. Because that's why she loved him so much. There were so many questions that she would ask him now. But there was no way to ask them now.
