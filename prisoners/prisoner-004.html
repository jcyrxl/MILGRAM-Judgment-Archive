MUU KUSONOKI

"You dislike Muu because of how blessed and privileged Muu is, so you gathered round and bullied Muu."

VIEW TRIAL RECORD ↓

BASIC INFORMATION

Muu is a popular foreign high school student and is labeled as the queen bee. She is privileged both at home and in school. She once was the bully but the tables turned on her and she became a victim.

PROFILE & ANALYSIS

Muu Kusunoki was born to a Japanese father and French mother, growing up with an overabundance of their love. Her beauty she got from her mother. This beauty attracted attention from a young age, and compounded with her privileged family situation had the makings of a life of many the envious, admiring eye being cast towards her as she grew up. It did not require for Muu to wish it so, she just always was at the center of every community.

At the time Muu entered high school, she was already so well known that there was not a soul in school who didn't recognise her. Male or female, everyone was attracted to her beauty, her aura. She wielded great influence just by existing. Muu had no need to put her desires into words, those around her preempted, and made sure she had nothing to desire for. Muu doesn't think this as strange, but as the natural state of things, and doesn't give this much thought. She was, in effect, the queen bee of her hive.

For the first time in her life, however, Muu encounters a person who doesn't bend to her will. This girl, a transfer student, did nothing to coddle Muu, nor did she show even an inkling of desire to get into Muu's good graces. She didn't treat Muu as special, just as another human being. The girl's actions caused Muu discomfort. Her entourage, as always, sensed this discomfort, and soon, bullying of the girl began.

The girl does not succumb, nor does she cry and apologise, and this caused the bullying to escalate. Muu gives no orders. She does not bring up the girl in conversation. Muu understood, however, that anyone who rebels against her must fall, and that she was undoubtedly the center of the universe.

The girl reaches a breaking point, and one day, she rams a chair into Muu's face in front of all their classmates. Muu could not understand; not the pain that she had felt for the first time, nor what was happening. She wailed, hideously, and she vomited from the shock. The beautiful Muu was no longer there, and instead, a unrecognisable version of her was exposed to all of her classmates. The revolution is realized, and their small society turns upside down.

Muu ends up at the receiving side of all the terrible bullying, formally directed at the girl. Muu could not comprehend the situation, nor was she able to ask anyone for help. She just stopped herself from feeling anything. The girl, newly freed from bullying, seemed to take absolutely no interest in Muu's predicament, only deigning her a single glance. In that instant, the anger that was lying dormant inside of Muu wells up — there can be no forgiveness for giving her that look. After school, Muu follows the girl, and pushes a utility knife inside of her body.
