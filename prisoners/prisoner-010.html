KOTOKO YUZURIHA

"Even if you tried to stop me, those who you wanted to protect still died."

VIEW TRIAL RECORD ↓

BASIC INFORMATION

Kotoko is quick-witted, and speaks up for herself. Due to her appearance, behavior and words, she may come off as cold, but her blunt attitude does not bear any ill will

PROFILE & ANALYSIS

Although reports are still underway, I believe Kotoko is a vigilante. She very clearly wants to persecute violence, focused on the more powerful who harm the weak. From the articles she looks through in the video, this means children, elderly and anyone else she doesn’t think can defend themselves. What we know for sure about Kotoko is that she was originally a college student studying law before dropping out and dedicating her life to being the "fang of justice." Some people speculate that the reason she's so obsessed with punishing people she deems to be evil is because she herself was victimized as a child but when asked about her background, she's pretty insistent that she had a relatively stable childhood. Her parents were alright, and she wasn't bullied in school or anything. In fact, she seems a bit miffed by the insinuation that only former victims can hate evil and that people can't hate it because it's the right thing to do. It's possible that she's lying or misleading us, but Kotoko has been very upfront about what she's all about, so it would be weird for her to suddenly start lying about this, especially when her wording of these answers is very frank. I really think it's as simple as Kotoko wanted to go into law to protect people, slowly became disillusioned with the whole thing the more she learned, and then dropped out to dish out her own brand of justice to the world. In regards to the case with child predator Kaneshiro, there's quite a bit we can glean about it from the article Kotoko is reading. The little girl Kotoko saved originally testified in court what Kotoko did was legitimate self-defense, but she later retracted that statement for unknown reasons. It's worth noting that Kaneshiro is actually the son of the president of a powerful newspaper company, and said president is trying to push the narrative that Kotoko went completely over the line, so I think the girl's family might have been financially coerced or worse into staying silent. Regardless, Kotoko still seems to have some fond feelings for the kid. Also, a lot of this is still speculation, but there are some people who think Kotoko isn't even here for the death of child predator Kaneshiro. There are some who think her victim could have been the little girl who might have been incidentally killed while others think it could've been for one of the other street criminals she targeted. All we know for sure is that she did indeed kill Kaneshiro, but there are some decent arguments to be made that she has more "victims" on top of that.
