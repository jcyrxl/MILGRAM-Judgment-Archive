BASIC INFORMATION

In contrast with her age, she is very calm, mature and polite. She is usually seen with a vague smile and hasn’t shown any sign of fear from finding herself in Milgram. Her words and actions are mature, but on the other side, her strong belief in God shows a childlike pureness.

PROFILE & ANALYSIS

Before she was born, her father's company nearly went bankrupt after being betrayed by a colleague of his, experiencing a V-shaped recovery shortly after his conversion to the religion. As a result, he dedicated his life to the faith, and raised Amane into it as well. Her whole life would since be devoted to the religion.

Amane's religion had a very strict, abnormal doctrine which did not align with conventional societal norms. She was told by her father her existence was a result of his miraculous encounter with the faith, as he had contemplated death before converting. In this religion, medicinal and recreational practices were a sin, the latter principle being very difficult for younger Amane to handle. Whenever she went against the religion's doctrine, she was met with verbal abuse and corporal punishment at the hands of her parents, with methods including cold water being poured onto her, battery with a wooden stick, and tasing, among other kinds of physical torture. These punishments greatly affected Amane's early development and perception of the world, obediently following her parents and their teachings without question.

Amane's father had eventually given up on his business to rise in the hierarchy of the religion, travelling throughout Japan to do so. Amane had spent most of her time home alone with her mother, and they were both proud of her father for his will to aim higher, despite the difficulties they may have faced. Eventually, Amane's mother discovered she has fallen terminally ill. Due to the faith's principle against medicine, she kept it as a secret from her husband, so as to not intervene with his progress with the faith. Over time, she got progressively weaker, and all she and Amane were able to do was to pray for her recovery. Her illness eventually became so severe to the point of collapse, and in a moment of desperation, she asked Amane to call for a doctor. This statement provoked Amane deeply and she began to punish her mother using the same methods her mother had previously used whenever she broke the cult's doctrine.

As a result of the injuries, her mother died, which deeply broke Amane's heart, but because of how deep her belief in the religion was rooted, she felt as though it was a necessary and justified procedure. She wholeheartedly believed her father would praise her for the course of action she took.
