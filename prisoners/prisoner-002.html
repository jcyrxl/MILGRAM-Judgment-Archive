YUNO KASHIKI

"Being forgiven or not forgiven... that's just child's play."

BASIC INFORMATION

Yuno is a high school student who is popular with her cute looks. Alothough liked by many boys her age, there are rumors spreading that Yuno seems to like, or date, older men more. Some say it's for the money because she's struggling financially, others state that Yuno just likes messing with them; neither of them are true. Yuno doesn't care about any of those, she just likes using them for her own good.

PROFILE & ANALYSIS

Yuno grew up in an average household, and was what could be considered a model student during her school years. She is friendly, pleasant, well-behaved, and has a good reputation. She displays absolutely no sign of problematic behavior. Once entering high school, she started to engage heavily in what was then called "sugar dating". Yuno finds little value in morality and ethics of sexuality, and ultimately offers up her body in exchange for money. There was nothing in her upbringing, however, that could possibly be the catalyst for such behavior. Her objective was not money. The money she gained from the sugar daddies she spent with her friends. She saved none of it. She does not think her situation as peculiar at all, but that "it just ended up being that way". Yuno had been skilled at reading people from a young age. She knew instictively what she should say and and how she should act to make the adults around her happy. The reality of this society, made up of what people say and what they actually mean bored and wearied her immensely, gradually robbing her body of its warmth. Yuno told no one, and no one caught on, that she has turned into ice. She keeps her activities secret, as they are socially considered reprehensible, but through them she is offered kindness, money, and warmth. Yuno could not understand what was so very wrong. Above all else, relationships made up of lies with people honest about their desires made everything a bit easier. One day, Yuno realizes that she is a host to a tiny new life. She had used protection, and she does not know who the father is. Inside her icy body, that new life burns her with its heat. What little warmth is left in her body, she feels being robbed away, She can sense her body getting even colder. It is not hard to imagine what her path will look like, that society will devour her whole and tear her apart. With her thoughts in disarray, Yuno loses conciousness and falls down a flight of stairs. The mother survives, and the new life is lost.
