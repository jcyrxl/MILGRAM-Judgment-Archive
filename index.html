THE JUDGMENT IS YOURS.

Ten prisoners. Ten crimes. And one question that has been placed in our hands: can they be forgiven? The prisoners have been brought here with little understanding of why they are being held inside MILGRAM. As their stories begin to unfold, fragments of their past, their relationships, and the crimes they committed are slowly revealed to us. But knowing what happened is not always enough. Some stories may make us sympathize with them, while others may leave us questioning whether forgiveness should even be considered. This archive documents the information revealed throughout the Trials, the clues hidden within their music videos, and the theories that attempt to make sense of it all. Listen carefully. Look closely. Then decide.
